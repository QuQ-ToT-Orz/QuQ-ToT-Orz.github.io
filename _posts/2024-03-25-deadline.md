It's common to struggle with procrastination and feeling lazy when facing a deadline. Here are some tips to help you deal with it:

1. Break down the task: Divide the project into smaller, manageable tasks. This can make it feel less overwhelming and easier to get started.
2. Set specific goals: Set clear and achievable goals for each day leading up to the deadline. This can help you stay focused and motivated.
3. Create a schedule: Allocate specific blocks of time to work on the project each day. Having a schedule can help you stay on track and prevent last-minute rushes.
4. Eliminate distractions: Identify and remove any distractions that might be hindering your productivity, such as social media or TV. Consider using tools like website blockers to help you stay focused.
5. Reward yourself: Give yourself small rewards for completing tasks or meeting milestones. This can help motivate you to keep going and make the process more enjoyable.
6. Seek accountability: Share your deadlines with a friend, family member, or colleague who can help keep you accountable. Having someone check in on your progress can provide extra motivation.
7. Practice self-care: Make sure to take breaks, get enough rest, and engage in activities that help you relax and recharge. Taking care of your physical and mental well-being can improve your productivity.

Remember, it's important to be kind to yourself and recognize that overcoming procrastination is a process. By implementing these strategies and being patient with yourself, you can improve your ability to meet deadlines effectively.
